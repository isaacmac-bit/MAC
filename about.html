Passenger profile

I've read a thousand rejection emails. Now I stop people from getting them.

I'm a job search strategist and interview coach working with people applying for remote roles from anywhere in the world — and running up against the same broken hiring pipelines everywhere.

How I got here

I spent years on the hiring side — screening resumes, running interview loops, and watching strong candidates get filtered out by keyword-matching software before a human ever saw their name.

Eventually I switched sides. I now work directly with candidates, especially ones applying for remote roles from outside the country where the company is based, where the odds are stacked even higher.

Who I work with

Job-seekers anywhere in the world targeting remote-first roles — engineers, designers, marketers, operators, and analysts who are good at their jobs but stuck getting past the application stage.

Most clients come to me after months of applying with little response. Some come earlier, wanting to do it right from the start.

How a booking works

Boarding
